Make the impulsive opens harder

Long-press any app for friction, not a warning — a pause, a block, or a colour drain, depending on what you set.

  • Wait timerA short pause before an app opens, to break the muscle-memory reach for it.
  • Block & schedulesLock an app out for a set time, or on a recurring schedule — can't be skipped.
  • GrayscaleDrains the colour from one app while you're in it, everywhere else stays normal.
  • Time reminderPick a session length; when it's up, choose Exit or Extend.
  • Shorts isolationYouTube Shorts get tagged Waste separately from long-form video.
  • Quiet inboxDistracting notifications land in Zen's own inbox instead of your status bar.

Private by design

No analytics. Nothing leaves unless you say so.

Every permission below is optional, requested only when you turn on the feature that needs it — and each one does less than you'd guess.

Everything lives on your device

Usage stats, tags, the age meter — all in a local database on your phone or PC.

Sync is opt-in and off by default

Nothing leaves the device until you sign in and turn on sync in Settings.

No analytics, no Zen account

No tracking SDK and nothing sent anywhere just to watch how you use the app.

Usage accessOptional+

Powers today's timeline and screen-time totals by reading Android's standard usage stats — how long each app was in front, never what was on screen.

AccessibilityOptional+

Powers the wait timer, blocks, grayscale and unlock rewards. Window-content reads happen in exactly two places: in Chrome, Zen reads the address bar and keeps only the domain, never the full URL; on YouTube, it checks view IDs — never text — to spot the Shorts player. Everywhere else it only sees which app is in front, never what you type or read.

Notification accessOptional+

Routes distracting notifications into Zen's own quiet inbox, and reads YouTube's now-playing notification to time watch sessions. Saved notifications are capped at 200 and excluded from backups.

Write secure settingsOptional · one-time (ADB)+

A single one-time adb command enables per-app grayscale. Skip it entirely if you don't use that feature.

Questions

FAQ

Is Zen free?

Yes — free to download and use, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Does any of my data leave my device?

By default, no. Everything lives in a local database on your phone or PC. Data only leaves if you turn on Drive sync — and even then it goes to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, never a server either of us runs.

Why does Android call Zen an "unknown developer"?

It just means the app isn't distributed through the Play Store — Zen needs permissions the Play Store's policies don't allow for this kind of app, so it's a direct APK download instead. That warning is expected for any sideloaded app.

Does Zen work if I don't set it as my home screen?

Yes. In app mode it runs alongside your current launcher and still tracks, tags, and shows the age meter — you just skip the extra friction of Zen's home screen replacing your app grid.

I have an iPhone or a Mac — can I use Zen?

Not yet. Zen is Android and Windows only today, with no timeline for iOS or macOS.

How do I update Zen?

Android: install the latest Zen.apk over the existing app. Windows: run the latest ZenDesktopSetup.exe — it upgrades your install in place.
