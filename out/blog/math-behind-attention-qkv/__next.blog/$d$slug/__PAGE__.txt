<p>Three matrices and four operations. The names are the confusing part, so start there:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Query</strong> — what this token is looking for.</li>
<li><strong>Key</strong> — what each token offers, as an advertisement.</li>
<li><strong>Value</strong> — the information a token actually hands over when chosen.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each token compares its query against every key to decide how much attention to pay, then collects a weighted blend of the values. The rest is arithmetic, and it is worth doing once by hand.</p>
<h2 id="words-become-vectors"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#words-become-vectors">Words become vectors</a></h2>
<p>Take three tokens: <code>birds</code>, <code>eat</code>, <code>seeds</code>. Each becomes a vector — here four numbers each, kept small so the arithmetic stays readable.</p>
